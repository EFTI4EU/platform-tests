Checklist of Platform-Internal eFTI Requirements

An informative checklist for eFTI platform operators, i.e. the eFTI service providers and economic operators legally responsible for the appropriate functioning of an eFTI platform (Art. 2(4) of Impl. Reg. (EU) 2025/2243), and for the developers of their platforms.

1Purpose and scope

An eFTI platform has to meet the requirements of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2243 and of Regulation (EU) 2020/1056 (the eFTI Regulation). What is observable via the machine-to-machine interface to an eFTI Gate (secure connection, registration of identifiers, data conformance, request/response cycles, and follow-up communication) can be checked by the automated test suite of this repository (see the test case description).

This checklist lists all other requirements, which cannot be tested from the outside: user identification and management, user-facing functions, signatures, logging, storage, retention and deletion, transparency duties, and organisational obligations.

Informative only: this checklist is an unofficial reading aid for the platform operator's own review. It does not replace the legal texts, and nobody verifies the ticked items. Responsibility for meeting the requirements remains entirely with the platform operator.

2How to read this checklist

  • The checklist is grouped by feature area as you would implement it (connections, user management, data set handling, logging, storage, notifications, user interface, and DIWASS).
  • The reference line of each item names the underlying legal provisions and, as "Draft DA: R-…", the corresponding specified requirements of Annex I of the draft delegated act on the certification of eFTI platforms (draft of 21 October 2026 on the EU's "Have Your Say" platform); the requirements map shows this mapping at a glance. Draft requirements not referenced by any item (R-G1-03, R-G2-02 to -05 and -07, R-G4-04 (except (iii), see LOG-01), R-G4-10, R-G4-11 (except (iii), (iv), see LOG-01), the Gate-observable parts of R-G4-12, and the Part C data requirements R-D1 to R-D3) are covered by the automated test suite instead; items without a "Draft DA:" reference have no counterpart in the draft, which focuses on the platform software, and are mainly relevant for service providers.
  • Article references without further specification refer to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2243 (technical specifications for eFTI platforms); "Annex" means its Annex.
