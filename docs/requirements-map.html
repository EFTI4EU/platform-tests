Requirements of the Draft Delegated Act on the Certification of eFTI Platforms — Mapping to Test Cases and Checklist Items

Specified requirements of Annex I of the draft delegated act on the certification of eFTI platforms (draft of 21 October 2026 on the EU's "Have Your Say" platform) and the test cases of the automated test suite and items of the requirements checklist of this repository that address them. The colours show the evaluation method the draft assigns to each requirement. Click a requirement, test case, or checklist item to highlight its connections; click it again or press Esc to reset.
